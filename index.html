Help & guide

How each part works.
Creating a job & paperwork
Tap + New Job. Step through Customer → Vehicle (size auto-detects from make/model) → Condition (tap the boxes + add photos) → Services (price follows the vehicle size) → Team checklist → Waiver (finger signature) → Delivery (signature at pickup). Save it. Reopen any job to print a Customer PDF, print the internal Team sheet, or email a receipt.
Cloud login & multiple devices
Under Cloud sync & login, enter an email + password once to create your owner login. Sign in with the same email on the iPad, phone, and computer and they all share the same jobs and customers. The app still works offline; it syncs when back online.
Customers
On Step 1 of a job, search a name or phone under "Returning customer" to auto-fill a repeat customer. Every customer is saved and shared across your devices.
Online booking & schedule
Set your open days/hours and slot length under Online booking, then share your booking link (see "Your links" above) on your website. Customer requests appear under "View booking requests" — Approve & confirm (makes a job + emails the customer), Suggest time, or Decline. A time only becomes unavailable to others once you approve a request.
Pricing
In the Pricing matrix, set each package's price for Compact / Standard / Full-Size. Set your new-customer discount %. These prices also drive the booking page.
Team & PINs
Add staff under Team & permissions with a name, role (Owner or Tech), and 4-digit PIN. Techs can run jobs but can't change pricing/settings or delete. Use "Lock / switch user" to hand the iPad to another person.
Email messages
Customize every message under Email messages using tags like {name} and {time}. The "Send outgoing messages" master switch turns all automatic emails off when you need to.
Reports
The 📊 Reports tab shows jobs, revenue, average ticket, new vs repeat customers, turnaround time, and where your customers come from.
QuickBooks & deposits
When QuickBooks is connected, completed jobs create an invoice and file the signed paperwork to the customer's QBO record. For deposits, paste a Square deposit link (made in your Square account) so customers can pay to hold a booking.
